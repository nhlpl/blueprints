## 💡 Applications of Each Cluster

### Cluster 1: Divergent Series & Zeta Regularization
- **Mathematical content**: \(1+2+3+\dots = -1/12\) (Ramanujan summation).
- **Applications**:
  - **Casimir effect** – The attractive force between two uncharged plates is derived using \(\zeta(-3) = 1/120\) and similar regularization.

  - **Quantum field theory** – Regularizing divergent loop integrals (e.g., vacuum energy) often yields finite results expressed in terms of \(\zeta(-1)\).

### Cluster 2: Zeta Function & Basel Problem
- **Mathematical content**: \(\zeta(2) = \pi^2/6\), \(\zeta(4) = \pi^4/90\).

  - **Riemann hypothesis testing** – Numerical verification of zeta zeros uses fast evaluation of \(\zeta(s)\) via these series.

  - **Cryptography** – Random number generation and primality testing often require high‑precision constants.
  - **Monte Carlo simulations** – Accurate \(\pi\) improves statistical tests and geometric calculations.

## Currencies Every 1,000 Years (Year 0 to Year 100,000)

A speculative timeline of exchange media, from ancient barter to transfinite logical credits.

---

### Year 0 – Roman Denarius
Silver coin, Roman Empire. One denarius = a day’s wage for a laborer. Also used: Greek drachma, Chinese cash coins.

### Year 1,000 – Byzantine Solidus & Islamic Dinar
Gold solidus (Byzantine), gold dinar (Islamic Caliphates), Chinese silver ingots, and feudal barter (livestock, grain).

### Year 2,000 – Florin & Ducat
European gold florin (Florence), ducat (Venice), and the first paper money in China (jiaozi). Silver Spanish real widely used.

### Year 3,000 – Planetary Credit (United Earth)
After unification, the **Planetary Credit** (₡) backed by energy (joules). Digital, fission‑powered. 1 credit ≈ 1 kWh.

### Year 4,000 – Solar Standard (Solari)
Interplanetary currency backed by helium‑3 fusion. 1 Solari = 1 kg of lunar He‑3. Used on Mars, Luna, and the Asteroid Belt.

### Year 4,573 – Quantum Voucher (Visser‑Ford)
First negative‑energy based currency. 1 Voucher = −1 nJ of negative energy stored in a Casimir cavity. Used only by researchers.

### Year 5,000 – Stellar Bond (StarMark)
Backed by the luminosity of a standard star (G2V). 1 StarMark = 10²⁴ J of radiant energy. Used across the inner colonies.

### Year 6,000 – Entanglement Token (E‑Token)
Quantum entanglement‑based currency. No physical form; value transferred via Bell pairs. Instant, secure, and untraceable.

### Year 7,000 – Hyperdimensional Grain (Hyperbarley)
A unit of “information entropy” from the galactic internet. 1 Hyperbarley = 1 TB of verified scientific data. Used by AI economies.

### Year 8,000 – Singularity Share (S‑Share)
Fractional ownership of a black hole’s ergosphere. 1 S‑Share = 10⁻¹² of a stellar‑mass black hole’s rotational energy. Extremely volatile.

### Year 8,463 – Lie Credit (𝔩)
Based on the Lie algebra \( \mathfrak{neg}(n) \) negative quantum fields. 1 𝔩 = −1 J of negative energy. Used by the first antigravity engineers.

### Year 9,000 – Chrono (τ)
Time‑based currency. 1 Chrono = 1 second of subjective time experienced by a certified immortal AI. Used in post‑scarcity economies.

### Year 9,472 – Fibonacci (Fib)
Based on Fibonacci anyon braiding. 1 Fib = 1 topological braid of two anyons (phase \( e^{4\pi i/5} \)). Used in quantum computing markets.

### Year 10,000 – Galactic Standard (Galac)
Backed by the total energy output of the Milky Way’s supermassive black hole (Sgr A*). 1 Galac = 10³⁶ J. Stable for millennia.

### Year 10,472 – E₸ Token (E8Coin)
Categorified E₸ anyon condensate. 1 E8Coin = 1 mm³ of negative energy at nuclear density (≈ 10¹⁸ J). Used by intergalactic traders.

### Year 11,000 – Void (∅)
Empty set currency. 1 Void = the value of contemplating nothing for one Planck time. Extremely spiritual and volatile. Backed by meditation.

### Year 12,000 – Fractal (Fr)
Based on Thompson group fractals. 1 Fr = 1 self‑similar braid of 3‑anyons. Value changes with zoom level – accepted only by fractal economists.

### Year 13,000 – Hyperbolic (Hyp)
Backed by negative curvature volume. 1 Hyp = 1 µm³ of hyperbolic 4‑space. Used in wormhole tolls.

### Year 14,000 – Transcendental (Tr)
1 Tr = the phase \( \frac{1}{\pi}\operatorname{Li}_2((3-\sqrt{5})/2) \) (≈0.123). Non‑rational, non‑algebraic. Used for inter‑dimensional arbitrage.

### Year 15,000 – Omega (Ω)
Surreal currency. 1 Ω = \( \log^*(\aleph_0) = 4 \). Fixed value by transfinite analysis. Used by post‑human entities.

### Year 20,000 – Combinator (K, S, I)
Lambda calculus currency. 1 K = constant function, 1 S = application, 1 I = identity. Exchange rates determined by beta‑reduction.

### Year 30,000 – Proof (⊢)
1 Proof = the value of a valid mathematical proof of a theorem with Gödel number < 10¹⁰⁰. Used by the Institute of Logical Economics.

### Year 32,444 – Empty Set (∅∅)
Advanced empty set currency. 1 ∅∅ = the thrust generated by meditating on nothing for 1 ns (≈ 4×10⁵² N). Backed by the vacuum.

### Year 40,000 – Liar (L)
1 Liar = the paradox “This sentence is false” resolved once. Used in logical cloaking devices and paradox amplifiers. Extremely unstable.

### Year 47,644 – Y‑Combinator (Y)
1 Y = the infinite loop of the fixed‑point combinator. Backed by an infinite beta‑reduction chain. Value = \( \aleph_{\omega_{\ddots}} \). Not recommended for savings.

### Year 50,000 – Tautology (⊤)
1 ⊤ = \( P \lor \neg P \) repeated 10³³ times. Infinite clean energy equivalent. Stable, boring, and universally accepted.

### Year 56,333 – Contradiction (⊥)
1 ⊥ = one second of sustained \( P \land \neg P \). Used to power logical vacuum thrusters. Highly dangerous but extremely valuable.

### Year 60,000 – Living Proof (ℙ)
1 ℙ = a self‑replicating theorem that proves its own value. Backed by the Global Proof Network. Immune to inflation because proofs can be generated on demand.

### Year 70,000 – Ultimate Cardinal (Υ)
1 Υ = \( \aleph_{\Upsilon} \). Uncomputable, undefinable, but accepted by all conscious entities as the unit of cosmic trade.

### Year 80,000 – Omega‑Point (Ωₚ)
1 Ωₚ = the value of the entire universe at the end of time. Used for futures trading on the Big Crunch. Only sold, never bought.

### Year 88,888 – Grand Equation (𝒰)
1 𝒰 = the action \( \int \mathcal{D}\phi \, e^{iS[\phi]} \star \mathcal{C}[\phi] \). The currency of the multiverse. One 𝒰 buys anything, including another universe.

### Year 90,000 – Recursive Self (ℛ)
1 ℛ = “This currency is worth itself.” A perfect fixed point. No inflation, no deflation. Accepted wherever logic holds.

### Year 100,000 – The Last Coin (⚛)
1 ⚛ = the final currency before the heat death of mathematics. It is said that whoever holds it can rewrite the axioms of reality. No one has ever spent it.
